# RMIT Global Mobility section map

| Route or ID | Title | Scenes |
|---|---|---|
| `index.html` | Home | Campus landing, service selection |
| `section-01` | Your Renewal Journey Begins | Opening, employee email, envelope, example calendar |
| `section-02` | Prepare Your Document | Opening, seven-tab organizer, document previews, closing, handover |
| `section-03` | Processing and approval | Opening, documents received, application prepared, submitted, under review, approved |
| `section-04` | Renewal Complete | Opening, three forms, two renewed documents, employee receiving them |
| `section-05` | Closing | Logo reveal, office farewell, centered message and Contact link |
| `contact.html` | Contact | Campus and working bilingual local FAQ with suggested questions and related guide links |

The five numbered stories are implemented in sequence in `renewal.html`. Repatriation is listed as Coming soon and has no route. The local FAQ requires no API key. The optional classifier remains unconfigured. Contact and Mobility Chat link to mobility@rmit.edu.vn.


- Section 01 uses a continuous office scene. The monitor email and the envelope both lead to the example calendar.
- Section 02 uses a red outer organizer and two independently animated receiving arms.
- Section 03 layers the progress trackers over the full-width office and reserves tracker space around the animated artwork and places the supplied transparent submission illustration on the surrounding blue tint.
- Section 04 uses RMIT Red through the forms, document reveal and single employee handover.
- Section 05 brings the two waving characters closer to the centered farewell.
- A browser refresh on any route returns to English Home at the top and clears temporary progress.
